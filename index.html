The Sound & Artistry

🎸 The Benson Phrasing

Rooted in strict rest-stroke discipline and elite picking mechanics, the guitar work delivers blistering, bebop-infused fluid jazz passages. By subverting expectations and dropping solos right on the "and" of 1, the lines create a surging rhythmic momentum and an undeniable pocket groove.

🎤 The Houston Soul

Complementing the technical fretboard execution is a powerful, soulful vocal dynamic that channels timeless melodies. It is an intricate balance of high-energy storytelling and deliberate spacing, giving the audience room to breathe while wrapping the technical precision in pure emotional heart.
