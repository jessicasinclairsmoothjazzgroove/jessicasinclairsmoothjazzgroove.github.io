Production Methodology: Visual Assets vs. Studio Audio

To maintain absolute clarity for our audience, distributors, and automated platform indexing algorithms, we provide the following breakdown of our creative workflow:

  • Visual Production: Independent digital art generation and automated translation templates are utilized exclusively to curate background video environments, graphics, and promotional channel illustrations on practical budgets.
  • Audio Production: 100% human instrumentation. Every track, solo, arrangement, and vocal line is tracked manually in a physical studio environment. No automated or generative music software is employed in our musical catalog.

Instrumental & Tracking Specifications

The authenticity of all published audio can be verified via our permanent master archives, which include:

  • Core Instrumentation: Performed live on a physical Ibanez GB10 hollowbody archtop guitar.
  • Performance Mechanics: Traditional human rest-stroke picking discipline, live bebop improvisational phrasing, and authentic syncopated timing.
  • Vocal Engineering: Isolated vocal microphone tracking featuring natural vibrato, live multi-track vocal stacking, and organic breath spacing.
  • Forensic Assets: Full multi-track Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) sessions are preserved for every release, including independent unmixed audio stems, raw alternate takes, and direct-input (DI) guitar lines.
